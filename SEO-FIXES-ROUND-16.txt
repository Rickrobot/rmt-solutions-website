PLANNING BASIS (deliberate)
  A forklift is not a crane, so BS 7121 and its lift categories are NOT
  used. Both pages and the template rest on LOLER 1998, ACOP L113,
  ACOP L117 and the manufacturer's instructions. Every capacity figure is
  taken from the manufacturer's data for that truck and attachment — the
  template tells the user not to estimate a reduced capacity by formula.

FACTS CHECKED AGAINST HSE / CPCS
  - Forklift lifting = lifting equipment under LOLER; also work equipment
    under PUWER (brakes, steering, tyres).
  - Thorough examination of chains, forks, mast/cylinders and attachments
    every 12 months or per a written scheme; accessories and people-lifting
    every 6 months (HSE lifting FAQ and thorough examination pages).
  - ACOP L117 Rider-operated lift trucks (2013).
  - CPCS A16 = industrial counterbalance forklift; A14 = rough-terrain
    masted forklift.
  - People never lifted on forks; non-integrated platforms only in the
    exceptional circumstances in HSE guidance.
