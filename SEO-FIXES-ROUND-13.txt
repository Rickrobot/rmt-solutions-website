META DESCRIPTION SWEEP

characters, so Google was truncating them mid-sentence in the results — and on
several the part being cut was the most persuasive bit of the sentence.

6 files changed. Verified against a real `next build`: 82 pages, compiled
clean.

NO CONTENT CHANGED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Only meta descriptions were touched. No body copy, no headings, no schema, no
internal links. Nothing on any page looks different to a visitor — this
affects only what appears under the blue link in Google.

Because of that, expect no ranking movement from this round. What it should
move is click-through rate on the pages that already rank, which is the
cheapest traffic available: no new content, no new links, just not throwing
away the last third of the sentence.
