1. FOUR BLOG TITLES + METAS REWRITTEN
======================================================================
These four URLs are 51% of all site impressions and convert at 0.54% CTR
(55,594 impressions -> 302 clicks). They rank well but nobody clicks,
because AI Overviews now answer definitional questions above the results.

Fix: stop promising an ANSWER, start promising an ARTEFACT that a summary
box cannot deliver. Every promise below is backed by an asset that already
exists on the page — no clickbait.

  /blog/what-is-bs-7121-complete-guide       8,685 imp, pos 7.3, 0.62% CTR
    OLD: What is BS 7121? UK Guide to Safe Use of Cranes

    NOTE: originally proposed "+ Free Compliance Checklist" — dropped,

  /blog/cpcs-appointed-person-guide          9,417 imp, pos 13.0, 0.30% CTR

All four are under the 60-char SERP limit. `absolute` title handling on blog
posts is unchanged, so no brand suffix is appended.
