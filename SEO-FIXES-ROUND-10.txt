FIX 2 — INVISIBLE FAQ SCHEMA (GOOGLE POLICY BREACH)                 [DONE]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Worse than the audit reported. EIGHT pages were emitting FAQPage JSON-LD for
questions that appeared nowhere in the rendered HTML — 20 questions in total.
Google's structured data policy requires FAQ markup to reflect visible content;
markup-only FAQs are manual-action eligible.

Root cause: every affected page kept TWO independent copies of its FAQs — a
hand-maintained `faqSchema` object for the markup, and a separate hardcoded
array for the visible list. They had drifted.

Fixed properly rather than patched: on each page the visible list is hoisted to
a module-level `PAGE_FAQS` const and `faqSchema` is now GENERATED from it. They
cannot drift again, because there is only one list. Questions that existed only
in the schema were folded into PAGE_FAQS, so nothing was lost.

  app/page.js                       3 schema questions, none visible -> now 8,
                                    all visible (new FAQ section added)
  app/services/steel-erection       4 schema questions, none visible -> now 10,
                                    all visible
  app/services/lifting-operations-audit   4 invisible -> recovered, 8 total
  app/services/lorry-loader-lift-plans    4 invisible -> recovered, 8 total
  app/services/tower-crane                5 invisible -> recovered, 16 total
  app/services/lift-plan-checking         4 invisible -> recovered, 16 total
  app/services/mobile-crane-lift-plans    2 invisible -> recovered, 10 total
  app/services/aerial-site-surveys        1 invisible -> recovered, 8 total
  app/services/excavator-lift-plans       had TWO visible FAQ blocks with
                                          near-duplicate answers plus a
                                          schema-only question — merged into
                                          one 15-question block

Verified: 265 FAQ questions across the site, 0 not present in rendered HTML.

FIX 4 — THE LONDON DRIVE-TIME BUG                                   [DONE]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
app/locations/[slug]/page.js printed each city's travelMinutes and then an
UNCONDITIONAL sentence claiming "practical for same-day site visits". On London
(180 min), Bristol (180), Glasgow (210) and Edinburgh (240) that sold a three-
to four-hour drive as same-day attendance — and on London it flatly
contradicted the honest body copy immediately above it.

  - new SAME_DAY_THRESHOLD_MINUTES = 90 constant
  - under 90 min: same-day wording retained (Warrington, Manchester, Salford,
    Stockport, Trafford, Liverpool, Wirral, Leeds)
  - over 90 min: "scheduled around your critical lifts", with the honest point
    that the PLAN is still 24-48 hours wherever the site is
    (Birmingham, London, Bristol, Glasgow, Edinburgh)
  - new formatTravelTime() — "3 hours from London", not "180 minutes from
    London"

This was a credibility risk, not just an SEO one.
