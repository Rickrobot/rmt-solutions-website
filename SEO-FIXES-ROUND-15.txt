SEO FIXES — ROUND 15 (12 October 2026)
======================================

GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE: "Image metadata" structured data — 4 non-critical issues

  Missing field 'license'
  Missing field 'creditText'
  Missing field 'copyrightNotice'
  Missing field 'acquireLicensePage'

Verified against a real `next build`: compiled clean, and every ImageObject
with a contentUrl in the rendered HTML (14 drawings across 4 pages) now
carries all four fields plus creator.


WHERE THE ISSUES CAME FROM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
components/LiftDrawingExamples.js. The drawing galleries on

  /services/steel-erection            (3 drawings)
  /services/excavator-lift-plans      (3 drawings)
  /services/lorry-loader-lift-plans   (4 drawings)
  /services/telehandler-lift-plans    (4 drawings)

publish each drawing as an ImageObject with a contentUrl and a creator. That
is what puts an image into Google's Image metadata report, and Google then
expects the rights fields as well. They were not there.

No other ImageObject on the site has a contentUrl — the case study hero
images and the publisher logos use `url` only — so they are not part of this
report and were left alone.


WHAT CHANGED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
NEW   lib/imageRights.js
      One shared IMAGE_RIGHTS object: license, acquireLicensePage,
      creditText, copyrightNotice, creator, copyrightHolder. Spread it into an
      ImageObject only for images RMT made itself — never a client's,
      manufacturer's or third party's image.

NEW   app/image-licence/page.js   →  /image-licence
      The page `license` points at. States the terms (all rights reserved,
      linking is fine, reuse needs written permission) and has a
      #request-permission section, which is the acquireLicensePage target.
      Same layout and tone as /privacy.

MOD   components/LiftDrawingExamples.js
      Each drawing ImageObject spreads IMAGE_RIGHTS.

MOD   app/sitemap-feed/route.js
      /image-licence added (yearly, 0.3) so Google can crawl the licence page
      it is being pointed at.

MOD   components/Footer.js
      "Image Licence" link in the bottom bar. The bar now wraps
      (flex-wrap, gap-x-6) so five links fit at phone width.
