WHY THIS PAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
The SERP research found NO exact-match competition anywhere in the UK for:

  same day lift plan            urgent lift plan
  next day lift plan UK         emergency appointed person
  appointed person at short notice
  out of hours lift plan        weekend lift plan UK

Not "weak competition" — none. Nobody has built a page for these. They are
also the highest purchase-intent queries in the entire keyword set, because
nobody searches "same day lift plan" out of curiosity. We were already picking
up 6 of 10 results on some of them incidentally, via the cost guide and the
services pages. This is the dedicated landing page that should lock them.

Every competitor claims "fast turnaround" and not one of them gives a number.
We give a number, and the page is built around that.


HOW IT IS WRITTEN — AND WHY IT SAYS NO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
The page has a section listing the lifts that CANNOT be turned around same day
— tandem and multi-crane, blind lifts, lifts over live highways or railways,
lifts near overhead power lines, and mobile crane work needing verified ground
bearing pressure.

That is deliberate, not a hedge. The whole proposition here is credibility
under time pressure. A visitor to this page is deciding within about ten
seconds whether you are someone who knows what they are doing or someone who
will say yes to anything. Publishing the limits is what makes the yes
believable — and it pre-qualifies the enquiries, so the phone calls you get are
the ones you can actually help with.

It also protects you commercially. A plan produced fast and then rejected by
the reviewer on Friday morning has cost the customer the day they were trying
to save, and that is the enquiry that turns into a complaint.

Other deliberate choices:

  - phone number above the fold, twice more further down. Someone with a lift
    tomorrow should be calling, not filling in a form
  - "order before 2pm" in the hero badge — a concrete cut-off is more
    persuasive than "fast", and it manages expectation before the call
  - a "what to have in front of you when you call" section, with confirmed load
    weight called out as the one that matters. Urgent jobs stall on missing
    information far more often than on availability, so this section saves you
    time on every call it works on
  - an emergency-AP section covering the CPA hire trap — the most common reason
    someone suddenly needs an Appointed Person at 48 hours' notice
  - honest about site attendance vs plan delivery: a same-day PLAN is same-day
    anywhere, same-day ATTENDANCE is 90 minutes from Warrington. Those are
    different promises and the page does not blur them
  - standard pricing stated. No urgency surcharge, out-of-hours agreed on the
    call before work starts
