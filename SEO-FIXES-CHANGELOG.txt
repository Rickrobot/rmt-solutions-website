LOCATION PAGES — SERP INTENT RE-TARGETING (the strategic fix)
  The audit found that "Lift Plans Manchester" SERPs are dominated by
  elevator/passenger lift companies (Northern Elevators, Sheridan Lifts,
  Invalifts, etc.). Google interprets "lift plans + city" as elevator
  services, not crane lift planning.

  Changes to app/locations/[slug]/page.js:
    - All 12 metaTitles changed from
        "Lift Plans <City> | CPCS Appointed Person | RMT Solutions"
      to
        "Crane Lift Plan <City> | LOLER Appointed Person"
      (template still adds "| RMT Solutions" automatically.)
    - H1 changed from "Lift Plans in {city}" to "Crane Lift Plan in {city}"
    - Form serviceName changed from "Lift Plans — {city}" to "Crane Lift Plan — {city}"
    - Meta descriptions LEFT UNCHANGED — they already contain LOLER, CPCS,
      tower crane, mobile crane, etc. which signal crane intent strongly.
      Re-targeting at the title + H1 level is enough.

  Changes to app/locations/page.js (hub):
    - Title: "Crane Lift Plan Services Across the UK"
    - H1:    "Crane Lift Plan Services Across the UK"
    - Card link anchor text: "Crane lift plans {city}"
    - keywords array re-targeted to crane/LOLER terms

  Changes to components/Footer.js:
    - Footer location link anchor text changed from "Lift Plans <City>"
      to "Crane Lift Plan <City>" for all 9 footer city links.
      This matters: internal anchor text drives ranking signals, and
      consistent crane-prefixed anchors across the site will help Google
      classify the location pages correctly.
